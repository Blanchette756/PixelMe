## PixelMe — a free dynamic island for Windows

**Download `PixelMe-Setup-x.y.z.exe` below and run it.** No admin rights needed, and .NET is included.

> Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

### What's inside

- 🏝️ **Island** at the top of your screen: notch or floating style, opens with a click, spring animations
- 🎨 **Themes**: Black, Glass, Graphite, Light and **Wallpaper** (tinted by your wallpaper), with real acrylic blur
- 🎵 **Music** from any app: cover art, colours from the cover, wavy progress bar, seek, volume, synced lyrics
- 📋 **Clipboard history**: text, links, images and files, with pinning
- 📁 **File shelf**: drop files on the island, drag them out again, or **send them to your phone** with a QR code
- 🔔 **Reminders and timers** with snooze and repeat
- 🎤 **Ctrl+Alt+M** mutes your microphone in every app, with a call timer
- 🎚️ **Quick controls**: volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, power mode
- 🔊 **Volume and charging pop-ups** in the island
- 🧩 **Widgets**: clock, world clock, weather, battery, stopwatch, PC stats, to-do, photos
- 🔔 **Windows notifications** in the island
- 🤖 **AI agent alerts** for Claude Code and Codex
- 🎮 **Game strip** with CPU, GPU and RAM while a fullscreen app is open
- 🌐 **Web drawer** for pinned websites, plus **Twitch / YouTube live chat** and an **OBS REC** indicator
- 🧭 **Dock**: optionally replace the Windows taskbar (the taskbar comes back automatically when you turn it off)
- ⬆️ **Update check**: PixelMe tells you when a new version is on GitHub

### Requirements

Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or newer, or Windows 11, 64-bit.

### Known limitations

- FPS and temperatures are not shown in the game strip yet.
- The dock does not show tray icons.
- Night light and Focus open Windows Settings instead of switching directly.
